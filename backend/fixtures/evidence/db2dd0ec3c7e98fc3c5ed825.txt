a. This offering is from SBI Cards and Payment Services Limited (“SBICPSL”)

b. By availing this Welcome Offer benefit and by conducting subsequent financial 
transaction(s), the Cardholder provides consent to all terms and conditions 
herein, including the ones for sharing of all personal, transactional and other 
related information for the purpose of this Offer. 
c. As part of the product feature, a SimplyCLICK Cardholder will become eligible for 
the one time welcome gift of Amazon Pay Gift Card worth Rs. 500 only after 
paying the annual membership fee (first year) of Rs.499/- for the eligible year. 
d. For the purposes of this Offer, an eligible year is defined as the year in which the fee 
is paid for by the Cardholder. Example: In case a card account is opened on 1st June 
2015, the period for the eligible year would be 1st June 2015 to 31st May 2016. 
e. All eligible SimplyCLICK SBI Cardholders will be issued an Amazon Pay Gift Card 
(Redeemable on Amazon.in or its mobile application) worth Rs.500. Voucher Code 
will be sent on customer’s registered mobile number within 21 days from 
payment of membership fee of Rs.499. 
f. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, it is stipulated that the customer 
account should be in good standing to be eligible for Gift meaning that for any 
reasons the credit card should not be suspended, blocked, inactive to be eligible 
for gifts under this Program 
g. The validity for ADDING the Gift Card in Amazon Pay is up to 6 months from the 
date of issuance of Gift Card. SBI Card shall share expiry details in an SMS. 
Whereas, the Gift Card balance itself shall be valid for 12 months from the date of 
adding the Gift Card to Amazon Pay. 
h. Voucher cannot be redeemed in parts or redeemed for cash under any 
circumstances. 
i. The voucher is offered by partnership establishment under a commercial 
arrangement with the provider. SBICPSL has no liability towards quality of goods 
and or services received by the Cardholder redeeming the vouchers.

customer shall not accrue till such date that correct MIDs are duly approved & 
provided by partner brand to SBI Card.
