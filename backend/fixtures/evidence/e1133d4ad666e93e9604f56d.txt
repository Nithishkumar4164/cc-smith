Revision of AU Small Finance Bank Credit Cards Reward Points Usage Terms & Conditions
Below changes will be applicable on your AU Credit Card w.e.f 5th July’23
1. Zenith Credit Card: Cardholders can earn maximum of 40,000 total Reward Points in
a statement cycle with a maximum of 20,000 Reward Points on each Merchant
category.
2. Reward Points Redemption Fees: A nominal fees of Rs. 99 + GST will be levied on
every successful redemption. These charges will be applicable for all AU Bank Credit
Card variants.
3. Redemption of Gift card’s/Vouchers/E-vouchers: Value of each Reward Points will be
INR 0.20 if redeemed against Gift card’s/Vouchers/E-vouchers. This will be applicable
for all AU Bank Credit Card variants. For all other categories of redemption value of
each Reward Points will remain unchanged.
For queries, please contact us at any of the touchpoints given below:
• Write to us on: creditcard.support@aubank.in or creditcard.priority@aubank.in (for
Vetta & Zenith cardholders)
• Call Us on Toll free Number 1800 1200 1500
Warm Regards,
AU Credit Card
