The Annual Fee of the card is Rs. 499 + taxes. The Renewal Fee is Rs. 499 + taxes from second year onwards. Renewal Fee is reversed if annual spends for last year >= Rs. 1,00,000. Can I use my card internationally? 
Yes, you can use your SimplyCLICK SBI CARD at over 24 million outlets across the globe, including 3,25,000 outlets in India. You can use your card to make payments at any outlet that accepts Visa / Master Card. You can withdraw cash from over 1 million Visa or MasterCard ATMs across the globe. How can I apply for the card? 
You can apply for an SBI Credit Card through our Website sbicard.com or via our Chatbot ILA.
