ICICI Bank Rewards Programme
Your ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card rewards you with reward points every time you swipe it, be it for travelling, shopping, eating out or
watching movies.
Earn 2 reward points on every `100 spent on all purchases, except on fuel.
Under the ICICI Bank Milestone Rewards programme, get 2,000 reward points on spending `2,00,000 on your card and 1,000 reward
points each time you cross `1,00,000 spends thereafter in an anniversary year; maximum of 10,000 reward points per year.
You can also redeem your reward points by calling ICICI Bank Customer Care.
9 INDEX
BILL PAYMENT OPTIONS
Online Payment
Make online payments using the Bill Pay (for ICICI Bank Savings Account) or Click to Pay (for savings account with other banks) options. You can also
make your Credit Card payment through our Customer Care if you have an ICICI Bank Savings Account.
Auto-debit
Control when and how you make payments without ever missing one! Get the convenience of an auto-debit facility if you hold a Savings Account with
ICICI Bank and pay your bills directly through your account by simply giving a written instruction to debit the payment every month on a pre-set due
date. In case the due date falls on a Sunday or a holiday, the amount would be automatically debited the next working day.
Cheque/Draft
You may also deposit your cheque or Demand Draft favouring your ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card No. Number at any of the drop boxes located at ICICI
Bank branches. An ICICI Bank cheque will take 3 days to clear; a Non-ICICI Bank cheque will take a minimum of 5 days to clear.

BENEFITS FOR YOUR CONVENIENCE
Utility Bill Payment
Use your ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card to make utility bill payments from the comfort of your office or home through our Customer Care or
Internet Banking.
Interest-free Credit
Enjoy up to 48 days of interest-free credit on your purchases. This is applicable only if the outstanding on your Credit Card as shown in the
statement is settled in full by the due date. Interest free credit for up to 48 days is not applicable for cash advances.
Supplementary Cards
You can also share these card benefits with your family members and present up to two supplementary cards to your parents, spouse,
children or siblings above the age of 18 years.
Fuel Surcharge Waiver
This card gives you a complete waiver of 1% on fuel surcharge every time you refuel at HPCL pumps. The offer is valid on maximum spends
of Rs 4,000 per transaction at HPCL pumps. To avail of the offer, payment should be made by swiping your card on ICICI Merchant Services
swipe machines only.
E-mail Statements and Mobile Alerts
E-mail statements and mobile alerts are faster and more reliable ways to get information without postal delays and ensure easier access
while on the move. Save time and paper by subscribing to e-mail statements.
TThe ICICI Bank 3D Secure Service, verified by Visa is the second level of authentication for your online card purchases.
11 INDEX
ICICI Bank 3D Secure Service
Protects your card against frauds. As per RBI rules, you must now also enter your unique Verified by Visa (VbV) password PIN besides the
CVV/Expiry date while shopping online. This service is free and you may register your card by visiting www.icicibank.com, from the time you
receive your card. Each of your ICICI Bank Credit Cards must be registered individually.
Online Banking
Register for ICICI Bank Internet Banking at www.icicibank.com and you can view your monthly statement, follow payment status and make
bill payments along with a range of other online services.
ATM Withdrawals
You can withdraw cash in times of urgency from ATMs up to the available cash limit. For the updated list of ICICI Bank ATM locations, please
visit www.icicibank.com. While you are travelling overseas, you can withdraw cash from ATMs bearing Visa logos. The interest rate for cash
withdrawal will be as per the 'Annualised Percentage Rate' (APR) applicable on your Credit Card. Please refer to the Most Important Terms and
Conditions (MITC) to know the APR of your Credit Card. Your available cash limit will be Nil for the first 180 days.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION ON YOUR ICICI BANK CHIP CREDIT CARD
Your ICICI Bank Chip Credit Card comes with an embedded microchip which provides additional security against counterfeiting/duplication of
the card. It also provides an extra layer of security in the form of a Personal Identification Number (PIN). You will be required to enter your
4-digit PIN on the terminal for doing transactions at merchant outlets.
Your 4-digit PIN is being sent through SMS at your registered mobile number. In case of non-receipt of the PIN within 7 working days from
the date of receipt of your card, you may generate your PIN by calling our Customer Care.
The 4-digit PIN is not required for online transactions. For online transactions, you need to register your new Chip and PIN Card for 3D Secure
(Verified by Visa) at www.icicibank.com.
