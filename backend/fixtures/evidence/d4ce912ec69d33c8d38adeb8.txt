Terms and Conditions for First Year Free (FYF) Offer
Swiggy Ornge HDFC Bank Credit Card
• First Year Free offer on Swiggy Ornge HDFC Bank Credit Card is applicable for card
applications starting 15th April’26 -10th September’26
• This offer is NOT applicable for the customers who have given consent on the Non-LTF
variant while applying for Swiggy HDFC Ornge CC on the bank portal.
• This offer is only applicable for Swiggy Ornge HDFC Bank Credit Card.
• FYF conversion will be done for all eligible customers within 90 days from card open date for
digital applications and at the time of card setup for physical applications.
• First Year Free cardholders sourced during the mentioned period are eligible for product
feature benefits (cashback and welcome benefit) as applicable for the prized customers.
• The Offer is only applicable to customers applying for the Credit Card through HDFC Bank's
direct channels, including the sales teams, the HDFC Bank Website, or communication from
HDFC Bank via bank-owned channels. This Offer is invalid if the Credit Card was applied
through a DSA or a partner website or physical application.
• Issuance of the Credit Card and applicability of the FYF Offer are subject to the completeness
of verified customer data, including the declared company name and approval of the Credit
Card application.
• In the journey, applicant will see the actual price of the card and within the 90 days of card
open date the FYF pricing will get updated.
• This FYF Offer is for a limited period and is subject to change at the sole discretion of HDFC
Bank .HDFC Bank reserves the right, at any time, without prior notice and without assigning
any reason whatsoever, to add/alter/modify/change or vary all of these terms and
conditions or to replace, wholly or in part, this offer by another offer, whether similar to this
offer or not, or to extend or withdraw it altogether at any time, without any prior notice
.
*Note - You may notice a fee of Rs.500 (+GST) for this card during the application process. This Fee
will not be charged & your card will become First-Year Free within 90 days of Card open date. Published on 12-08-2026
