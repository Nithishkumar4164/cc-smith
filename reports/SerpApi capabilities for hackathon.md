# Build big on 250 free searches

You get **no free or extra credits during the SerpApi India Hackathon 2026**. The only budget you can build with is the standard free account: **250 successful searches per month, capped at 50 per hour**. The **1,000 credits for each valid submission**, and the larger award credits, arrive only after judging, which has no announced date ([Hackathon site](https://serpapi.github.io/serpapi-india-hackathon-2026/), [Rules](https://serpapi.github.io/serpapi-india-hackathon-2026/rules.html)). That limit matters less than it sounds. SerpApi charges nothing for cached, errored or failed searches. The Account and Locations APIs are free. A repeated identical request within an hour costs nothing ([Pricing](https://serpapi.com/pricing), [Search API](https://serpapi.com/search-api)). So a disciplined five-day build fits inside 250 credits. In return you get one of the widest search-data catalogs available: about **115 `engine=` ids across 22 families**, roughly 70 of them Google. They cover web, AI answers, flights, hotels, maps, shopping, jobs, news, scholar, patents, trends, finance, YouTube and e-commerce ([llms.txt](https://serpapi.com/llms.txt)). India support is strong on every Google engine and on Amazon.in, Tripadvisor.in and the Apple stores, and weak or missing on eBay, Yelp, Walmart, Home Depot and Zillow. With the deadline at **Oct 10, 2026, 23:59 IST**, the useful questions are which engines fit your track, which SDK or agent integration saves you time, and how to plan your calls so you never hit HTTP 429 in the middle of your demo.

## Every hackathon credit arrives after you submit, not before

The hackathon site, rules, terms, submit form and config JavaScript all repeat the same message: "Create a free SerpApi account to get 250 search credits each month for building and testing your project" ([Hackathon site](https://serpapi.github.io/serpapi-india-hackathon-2026/)). The site's `hackathon-config.js` defines credits only for award tiers and the post-submission 1,000. It has no coupon, promo or boost field anywhere ([hackathon-config.js](https://serpapi.github.io/serpapi-india-hackathon-2026/hackathon-config.js)). The contact section lists adarsh@serpapi.com for hackathon and rules questions and contact@serpapi.com for API help. Neither address is offered as a way to request credits. The pricing page has no student, hackathon or open-source tier either ([Pricing](https://serpapi.com/pricing)). Community partners such as HydPy "are not responsible for administering the Hackathon... or paying awards" ([Terms](https://serpapi.github.io/serpapi-india-hackathon-2026/terms.html)). Two things online look like counterexamples, and neither applies here. The "USD 75 redeemable perks" figure comes from SerpApi-sponsored AI Tinkerers events in Paris ([AI Tinkerers Paris](https://paris.aitinkerers.org/p/ai-hackathon-real-time-search-powered-by-serpapi-hybrid)). The "Apives500" 500-credit code was a third-party giveaway from April 2026, limited to the first 30 people ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/giveaway-500-free-serpapi-credits-for-the-first-30-builders-a5a74b4ba4)). SerpApi clearly gives upfront credits at in-person sponsored events and chose not to for this online one. You can still politely email adarsh@serpapi.com if you run dry, but nothing documented entitles you to a top-up.

Credits after the event are worth having. The rules value credits at **100 per US dollar**, so the 1,000 participation credits equal $10 of usage. Every valid submission gets them, on top of any competitive award. The award table is: first place ₹1,00,000 plus 15,000 credits; second ₹40,000 plus 10,000; third ₹20,000 plus 10,000; best in each of the six tracks 10,000 credits; and best from each of five partner communities ₹10,000 plus 5,000 credits ([Rules](https://serpapi.github.io/serpapi-india-hackathon-2026/rules.html)). That totals **₹2,10,000 cash and 120,000 award credits**. The blog's "₹3 lakh+" headline most likely counts the credit value as well ([SerpApi blog](https://serpapi.com/blog/introducing-the-serpapi-india-hackathon-2026/)). A project can win **only one competitive award**. Overall placings are assigned first, then track awards, then community awards. Credits "have no cash value, are non-transferable... may expire" and depend on identity, residency, tax and banking verification ([Terms](https://serpapi.github.io/serpapi-india-hackathon-2026/terms.html)). The terms don't say how credits are delivered or when they expire. Since credits attach to an account, it is sensible to use the same email for your SerpApi account and for the lead participant on the submission.

A few rules can cost you more than any credit shortfall:

- **Submit before the deadline.** The window closes Oct 10 at 23:59 IST. The HydPy Meetup page still shows a stale Oct 5 date ([HydPy Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/hydpygroup/)). Drafts are "not submitted automatically", and a draft is never judged.
- **Repo and video.** You need a public GitHub repo with setup instructions and a screen recording under three minutes that shows the project running locally. The video link must open in an incognito window.
- **Meaningful SerpApi usage.** "Adding an isolated or cosmetic API call solely to meet eligibility requirements is insufficient" ([Rules](https://serpapi.github.io/serpapi-india-hackathon-2026/rules.html)).
- **No exposed secrets.** Exposing an API key in public materials is grounds for disqualification, and judges may read your repo history. Keep `SERPAPI_API_KEY` in a `.env` file that has never been committed.
- **Disclosures.** Declare any pre-existing project and list the AI tools you used. AI use does not affect judging.
- **Eligibility.** Teams can have up to five members, all **resident in India and 18 or older**.
- **Judging criteria.** Judges score idea strength, originality, technical complexity, usefulness and meaningful SerpApi usage, all unweighted.

## Seventy Google engines anchor a 115-engine catalog

Every call is a GET to `https://serpapi.com/search` with `engine`, `api_key` and engine-specific parameters. Every engine also accepts `no_cache`, `async`, `output` (`json`, `html` or `md`) and `json_restrictor` ([llms.txt](https://serpapi.com/llms.txt), [Search API](https://serpapi.com/search-api)). The catalog makes most sense grouped by the job it does, not as a list.

| Family | Key `engine=` ids | What comes back | Chaining / pagination |
|---|---|---|---|
| Web and AI answers | `google`, `google_light`, `google_ai_mode`, `google_ai_overview`, `bing_copilot`, `brave_ai_mode`, `search_index` (preview) | organic_results, knowledge_graph, related_questions, answer_box, ai_overview, events_results; AI Mode text_blocks, reconstructed_markdown, references | `start` offset; `ai_overview.page_token` expires in about 1 min; AI Mode `subsequent_request_token` for multi-turn ([AI Mode](https://serpapi.com/google-ai-mode-api), [AI Overview](https://serpapi.com/google-ai-overview-api)) |
| Travel | `google_flights`, `google_flights_deals`, `google_travel_explore`, `google_hotels`, `google_hotels_reviews`, `google_hotels_photos` | best_flights, other_flights, price_insights; hotel properties, prices, amenities | `departure_token` returns the return leg, `booking_token` returns booking options; `property_token` returns hotel details ([Flights](https://serpapi.com/google-flights-api), [Hotels](https://serpapi.com/google-hotels-api)) |
| Local and maps | `google_maps`, `google_maps_reviews`, `google_maps_photos`, `google_maps_directions`, `google_local`, `tripadvisor`, `apple_maps` | local_results, place_results, reviews, topics, directions, durations | Maps pages in steps of 20 via `start`; `place_id`/`data_id` feeds reviews, photos and directions ([Maps](https://serpapi.com/google-maps-api)) |
| Commerce | `google_shopping`, `google_shopping_filters`, `google_immersive_product`, `amazon`, `amazon_product`, `google_lens` | shopping_results (about 40), per-seller prices, specs, reviews, visual_matches | Shopping ignores `start`; `immersive_product_page_token` returns multi-seller detail ([Shopping](https://serpapi.com/google-shopping-api)) |
| Knowledge and public data | `google_jobs`, `google_news`, `google_scholar`, `google_patents`, `google_trends`, `google_finance`, `google_sports` | jobs_results, news_results, citations, patent claims, interest_over_time, quotes | Jobs uses `next_page_token` only ([Jobs](https://serpapi.com/google-jobs-api)) |
| Video and apps | `youtube`, `youtube_video`, `youtube_video_transcript`, `youtube_channel`, `google_play`, `apple_app_store` | video results, transcripts, comments, app ratings and reviews | `next_page_token` for comments and related videos ([YouTube video](https://serpapi.com/youtube-video-api)) |

The 2026 changelog shows a product moving fast toward AI use cases. Since January SerpApi has added **Brave AI Mode, the Google Ads API, Scholar Case Law, Tripadvisor Reviews, Apple Maps, Instagram Profile, Hotels Photos, AI Flight Deals, Google Sports, Amazon Autocomplete, Zillow, YouTube Channel and eBay Seller**, plus AI Mode's `image_url` input and Lens direct image upload ([Changelog Apr 13–19](https://serpapi.com/blog/serpapi-weekly-changelog-april-13-19-2026/), [Changelog Sep 07–13](https://serpapi.com/blog/serpapi-weekly-changelog-sep-07-13-2026.md)). The most useful addition for anyone feeding results to an LLM is **`output=md`**, which "works with every API" and uses about half the tokens of JSON ([Markdown output](https://serpapi.com/markdown-output)). The MCP README claims savings of over 90% on deeply nested responses ([serpapi-mcp](https://github.com/serpapi/serpapi-mcp)). Deprecations cut the other way. **Google Events stopped accepting requests in August 2026**, so an event finder should call `google` with a query like "events in Bengaluru" and read `events_results` ([Changelog Aug 24–30](https://serpapi.com/blog/serpapi-weekly-changelog-august-24-30-2026/)). `google_product` now returns an error, so use Immersive Product instead. `google_jobs_listing` returns only ratings. The Jobs engine no longer accepts `start`.

For India, every Google engine works with `google_domain=google.co.in`, `gl=in`, `hl=en` or `hl=hi`, and a `location` string. You get that string free from the Locations API (`/locations.json?q=Bengaluru` returns a `canonical_name` to pass back) ([Locations API](https://serpapi.com/locations-api), [Google domains](https://serpapi.com/google-domains.json)). Flights, Hotels, Travel Explore and Flight Deals accept **`currency=INR`** ([Travel currencies](https://serpapi.com/google-travel-currencies)). `amazon.in`, `www.tripadvisor.in`, the Apple stores' `country=in`, and Brave AI Mode all support India ([Amazon domains](https://serpapi.com/amazon-domains), [Tripadvisor domains](https://serpapi.com/tripadvisor-domains), [Brave countries](https://serpapi.com/brave-countries.json)). India is missing from eBay and Yelp, which have no Indian domain. Home Depot covers only the US and Canada, Walmart and Zillow are US-only, Scholar case law covers US courts, and the Flights `exclude_basic` filter works only on US domestic flights ([eBay domains](https://serpapi.com/ebay-domains), [Yelp domains](https://serpapi.com/yelp-domains), [Home Depot](https://serpapi.com/home-depot-search-api)). Some India coverage is still unverified: Amazon `delivery_zip` with Indian PIN codes, OpenTable in India, and Google Local Services Ads in India. Test these in the Playground before you build on them.

## Pick the engine your track rewards

The hackathon's own examples point at the intended engines. Its examples include comparing flights, hotels and places to eat; tracking one product's price across sellers; finding first developer roles through Google Jobs; and building a local event finder with Maps ([SerpApi blog](https://serpapi.com/blog/introducing-the-serpapi-india-hackathon-2026/)). The mapping below follows that intent and drops engines that don't serve India.

| Track | Primary engines | India settings | Why it scores |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI Agents | `google_ai_mode` (multi-turn), `google_light` (cheap grounding), `google_news`, `youtube_video_transcript`, `brave_ai_mode`, SerpApi MCP | `gl=in`, `output=md` | Multi-turn tokens and the free schema resources let an agent plan, search and drill down |
| Open-Source Integrations | Any engine via a new connector | n/a | Clear gaps: no TypeScript agent-tools package, LangChain wrappers still on the 2023 SDK, no LlamaIndex or Haystack SerpApi tool, no documented Ollama path |
| Travel & Local Discovery | `google_flights` → `departure_token`/`booking_token`, `google_hotels` → `property_token`, `google_travel_explore`, `google_flights_deals`, `google_maps` → reviews/directions, `tripadvisor` | IATA codes BLR/DEL/BOM, `currency=INR`, `tripadvisor_domain=www.tripadvisor.in` | Token chains add real technical depth, not one cosmetic call |
| Commerce & Market Intelligence | `google_shopping`, `google_shopping_filters`, `google_immersive_product`, `amazon`/`amazon_product`, `google_lens`, `google_trends` | `amazon_domain=amazon.in`, `google_domain=google.co.in` | Cross-seller price comparison and visual search match the track brief |
| Knowledge & Public Interest | `google_jobs`, `google_news`, `google_scholar`, `google_patents`, `google_trends`, `google_finance` | `location="Bengaluru, Karnataka, India"`, `geo=IN`, `country=IN`, `NSE:` tickers | Public-data verticals with obvious usefulness |
| Open Innovation | `google` (`events_results`), `google_maps`, `google_lens`, `google_sports` | as above | Combining two or three engines beats wrapping one |

The Open-Source track asks entrants to "build a plugin, provider, or connector for an agent framework, MCP client, or developer tool" ([Hackathon site](https://serpapi.github.io/serpapi-india-hackathon-2026/)). The researchers did not check whether community packages already fill these gaps, so search npm, LlamaHub and the Open WebUI tools before you commit. Separately, the BuiltWithSerpApi gallery already lists about **190 projects across 65 engines**, many of them Streamlit, n8n or CrewAI wrappers ([BuiltWithSerpApi](https://serpapi.github.io/BuiltWithSerpApi/)). That works against thin single-engine demos when judges score originality.

## Official SDKs, MCP and agent tools remove most plumbing

The recommended Python client is **`pip install serpapi`** (v1.1.2). It is synchronous, returns a dict-like `SerpResults` with `next_page()` and `yield_pages()` helpers, and raises `serpapi.HTTPError` with a status code ([serpapi-python](https://github.com/serpapi/serpapi-python), [models.py](https://github.com/serpapi/serpapi-python/blob/master/serpapi/models.py)). The trap: the legacy **`google-search-results`** package (last released March 2023) *also* installs a module named `serpapi`. LangChain-community's `SerpAPIWrapper` and its Jobs, Finance and Lens wrappers still import that legacy module, so mixing them with the new client in one environment breaks imports ([langchain-community source](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langchain-community/blob/main/libs/community/langchain_community/utilities/serpapi.py)). For JavaScript, `npm install serpapi` (v2.2.1) provides `getJson`, `getHtml` and `getMd`. It has **no built-in pagination**, and the old `google-search-results-nodejs` package is deprecated ([serpapi-javascript](https://github.com/serpapi/serpapi-javascript)). Official repos also exist for Ruby, Go, PHP, Java, Rust, .NET, Swift, C++ and a CLI ([Integrations](https://serpapi.com/integrations)). Keep calls server-side whatever the language: calling from the browser exposes your key and can fail on CORS ([Fix SerpApi errors](https://serpapi.com/blog/fix-serpapi-errors-guide.md)). One difference trips people up often. Query keys vary by engine: Google uses `q`, YouTube `search_query`, Amazon `k`, eBay `_nkw`, Yahoo `p`, Walmart `query` and the App Store `term`.

For agent builders, the fastest route is **`serpapi-search-tools`** (v1.0.0, Python 3.10+, MIT). The hackathon resources page names it directly. It provides nine tool constructors: web, news, maps, images, shopping, videos, hotels, flights and travel explore. It also detects whichever of **14 agent SDKs** is installed and returns that SDK's native tool type ([serpapi-search-tools](https://github.com/serpapi/serpapi-search-tools-python), [frameworks guide](https://serpapi.github.io/serpapi-search-tools-python/user-guide/frameworks.html)):

- OpenAI Agents SDK
- Pydantic AI
- LangChain
- LangGraph
- CrewAI
- LlamaIndex
- Claude Agent SDK
- Microsoft Agent Framework
- AutoGen
- Haystack
- Semantic Kernel
- Agno
- smolagents
- Google ADK

`web_search` defaults to the cheaper `google_light`. You can restrict which engines a tool may call with `allowed_engines`. Maps place details, reviews and directions are *not* covered, so you need direct calls for those. CrewAI's own `crewai_tools` SerpApi tools already use the new client ([crewAI source](https://github.com/crewAIInc/crewAI/blob/main/lib/crewai-tools/src/crewai_tools/tools/serpapi_tool/serpapi_base_tool.py)). Ollama is not documented anywhere. Because the frameworks are model-agnostic, swapping `ChatOpenAI` for `ChatOllama` with a model that handles tool calls well should work, but nobody has documented or tested it.

The **official MCP server** runs hosted at `https://mcp.serpapi.com/mcp`. You can authenticate with a Bearer header or put the key in the URL path. You can also self-host it with `uv run src/server.py`, run it over stdio, or install the Claude Desktop `.mcpb` bundle ([serpapi-mcp](https://github.com/serpapi/serpapi-mcp)). It exposes a single `search` tool that covers every engine (defaulting to `google_light`, with `mode` set to compact or complete) and per-engine parameter schemas at `serpapi://engines/<engine>`. Clients that support the July 2026 MCP spec get a form asking for missing required fields, such as flight dates, before any credit is spent. Two optional MCP Apps tools, `search_table` and `search_dashboard`, render results as UI "so the bulk SERP JSON never enters the model's context window". The README gives one-line setup for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code, OpenClaw and Hermes. Outside Python, the options are:

- **n8n:** a verified "SerpApi Official" node covering 22 APIs ([SerpApi blog](https://serpapi.com/blog/boost-your-n8n-workflows-with-serpapis-verified-node/)).
- **Coding agents:** SerpApi-authored plugins for Hermes, Claude, Cursor, Codex, OpenCode and OpenClaw ([SerpApi GitHub](https://github.com/serpapi)).
- **Vercel:** Vercel Connect handles credentials only. It is not an AI SDK tool ([Vercel Connect](https://vercel.com/connect/serpapi)).

## Caching and billing rules turn 250 searches into a full build

The billing model is simple once stated precisely. Only **successful** searches count. Cached, errored and failed searches are free ([Pricing](https://serpapi.com/pricing)). The cache lasts **one hour** and serves a response only when "the query and all parameters are exactly the same". `no_cache=true` bypasses it and is billed ([Search API](https://serpapi.com/search-api), [LangChain mirror of the docs](https://reference.langchain.com/javascript/langchain-community/tools/serpapi/SerpAPIParameters)). A query that returns *no results* still counts as successful and still costs a credit, because "the search engine is responsible for the content" ([SerpApi blog](https://serpapi.com/blog/calculating-search-credit-usage-with-serpapi/)). Every additional page costs a credit. Google stopped honoring `num=100` on Sept 11, 2025, so pages hold 10 results and the top 30 costs three credits ([SerpApi blog](https://serpapi.com/blog/minimum-amount-of-results-per-page-across-serpapis-supported-google-engines.md)). SerpApi built a `google_light_fast` workaround and then discontinued it once Google capped that endpoint at three results ([SerpApi blog](https://serpapi.com/blog/solution-to-scrape-100-search-results-on-google-google-fast-light-api.md)). When credits run out, or you go over 50 searches an hour, you get **HTTP 429** with "Your account has run out of searches." A 200 response can still carry an `error` field, so check both ([Status codes](https://serpapi.com/api-status-and-error-codes)). Async mode (`async=true`) returns a `search_id` you poll at `/searches/{id}.json`, and results stay retrievable for 31 days. The docs don't say whether archive reads are free ([Search Archive API](https://serpapi.com/search-archive-api)).

Over five days, 250 credits is about **50 a day**. A practical plan:

**Day 1: explore without spending credits.**
- Build every request in the Playground.
- Resolve every Indian city once through the free Locations API.
- Call the free Account API (`/account.json`), which reports `plan_searches_left`, `this_hour_searches` and `plan_renewal_date` without using quota ([Account API](https://serpapi.com/account-api)).
- If you signed up in an earlier month, check `plan_renewal_date`. The free allowance appears to reset monthly from sign-up, so a reset could fall inside the hackathon window. That is unconfirmed.

**Days 2–4: build against saved fixtures.**
- Capture one real response per engine and query shape, save the JSON to disk, and develop the parser, UI and agent logic against those files.
- Put a small server-side cache (SQLite or a JSON file) in front of every live call.
- Keep request building deterministic. Any change to `location`, `hl` or an SDK-added parameter breaks SerpApi's free cache match.
- Validate inputs before sending, so empty or garbage queries don't burn credits on zero-result searches.
- Prefer `google_light` for agent grounding, use `json_restrictor` or `output=md` to keep payloads small, and cap agents at a fixed number of tool calls per question.
- Use token chains (Flights → `booking_token`, Shopping → Immersive Product) only when the user asks for detail.

**Day 5: record the demo from warm cache.**
- Replay the exact queries within the hour, so the recording costs nothing and can't fail on a 429.
- Add a startup check that refuses to run live calls below a credit floor.

**If you still run short:**
- Each teammate can use their own legitimate account.
- Do not stack multiple accounts per person. The ToS lets SerpApi "limit quantities per person or household", and the hackathon disqualifies for "abuse of SerpApi services" ([Legal](https://serpapi.com/legal), [Rules](https://serpapi.github.io/serpapi-india-hackathon-2026/rules.html)).
- The paid fallback is the Starter plan: **1,000 searches for $25**, refundable within 7 days if you've used less than 20% ([Pricing](https://serpapi.com/pricing), [Legal](https://serpapi.com/legal)).

Some limits remain. The Free plan carries no Legal Shield, ZeroTrace or priority support. SERP content shifts with location, device and Google's experiments, so a cached snapshot also protects your demo from changing results. There are no official latency figures. One sample response took 0.87s, so plan the UI around a few seconds per uncached call ([Fix SerpApi errors](https://serpapi.com/blog/fix-serpapi-errors-guide.md)).

## Conclusion

The free tier works like a design constraint, and the projects most likely to score well treat it that way. Hard limits push you toward fixture-driven development, deterministic caching and cheap-engine-first agents. That same architecture makes a demo reliable, keeps your key out of the repo, and gives judges visible technical depth through token chains, not one cosmetic call. So "no extra credits" is less a handicap than a filter, and it favors well-engineered entries.

The strategic opening is in coverage gaps more than engine count. SerpApi's own integrations are Python-first and current, while JavaScript agent tooling, the LangChain-community wrappers, the LlamaIndex and Haystack tools, and local-model (Ollama) paths lag behind. Several 2026 engines (AI Mode multi-turn, Flight Deals, Hotels Photos, Google Sports, YouTube Channel) have barely any showcase projects yet. Pairing one of those newer engines with an India-specific angle (INR fares, amazon.in, google.co.in jobs, NSE tickers) gives you originality and usefulness together, and those are two of the five equally weighted judging criteria.
