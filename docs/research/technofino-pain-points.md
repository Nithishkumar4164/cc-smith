# TechnoFino community — pain-point sweep (Oct 2026)

## Method and caveats
- technofino.in sits behind a Cloudflare challenge (direct fetches returned 403). Threads were read via Wayback Machine snapshots with a parser for XenForo post bodies, plus search snippets for threads not archived.
- 36 distinct threads read in full or in part (first ~20 posts of the snapshot); ~40 more cited from search snippets/titles only (marked "snippet").
- Snapshots are dated 2023–2025, so the most recent pages of hot threads were not visible.
- Frequency: "very common" = seen across many banks/threads and repeatedly re-asked; "common" = several threads; "occasional" = one or two threads.

## Cross-cutting: merchant / MCC opacity
Users cannot know at payment time what MCC a merchant will send, so they cannot know if the transaction earns, counts toward milestones/waivers, or attracts a surcharge. Banks disclaim liability for acquirer MCC errors. https://technofino.in/community/threads/what-is-mcc-why-is-it-important-consequences-of-merchants-using-the-wrong-mcc.17170/

## Top 10 by frequency
1. Rewards / cashback / milestones not credited, no transaction-level visibility, PNO escalation needed.
2. Constant devaluations and T&C changes communicated poorly.
3. "Which card should I get / which card for this purchase" — especially rent, utilities, insurance, fuel, UPI, wallet loads.
